## Choose safe defaults

Secure-by-design systems make the safe action the easy action. Use least-privilege access, deny-by-default policies, short-lived credentials, and clear ownership boundaries as defaults rather than optional hardening.

## Reduce attack surface

Remove unused endpoints, scopes, dependencies, and data retention. Every capability that does not exist is one less capability that must be protected, monitored, and explained.

## Design for recovery

Assume controls can fail. Add useful audit records, revocation paths, rate limits, and tested recovery procedures so the team can contain harm quickly.
