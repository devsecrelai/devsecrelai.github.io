## Establish a shared rhythm

Name an incident lead and communications lead. Set a predictable update cadence so responders can focus while stakeholders know when to expect the next status.

{% include toc.html %}

## State facts and decisions

Explain the impact, affected users, current mitigation, and next decision point. Avoid speculation; label uncertainty clearly and update the record as facts change.

## Learn in the open

After recovery, publish an appropriate summary of what happened, what worked, and which systemic improvements will follow. Blameless learning strengthens trust and resilience.
